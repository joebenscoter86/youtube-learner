# After Effects add-on

For After Effects tutorials, the learner can prove a recipe by building it in After Effects itself, through the Comp Room plugin (https://github.com/joebenscoter86/after-effects-studio). Without Comp Room, After Effects recipes still get written and checked; their proof is a try-it card the person follows by hand.

- `prove.md`: how a builder proves a recipe in After Effects. `prompts/prove.md` points here when the tools are available.
- `prelude.jsx`: small ExtendScript helpers the proof scripts load.
- `example-dotted-ring.jsx`: a proof script that has run, as a model.
